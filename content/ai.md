Short version: **topics, lab work, experiences and opinions are mine. AI helps me with draft, writing, editing and illustrations. I review everything and I take responsibility for what is published here.**

Every post that was written with AI help carries a small *AI-assisted* label next to the date and a short note at the end. This page explains what it means.

## What is mine

- **Choice of topics.** What I explore and write about comes from my own work, curiosity and 25+ years with enterprise mail.
- **Lab work.** Servers, configurations, tests, captures, mistakes and fixes - I build and run them myself.
- **Experiences and opinions.** Comparisons with Exchange, what worked, what bit me, what I think about it - that is my view, not a model's.
- **Notes and skeletons.** Most posts start as my own notes, bullet points and half-sentences (sometimes in Polish).

## What AI does

I work mostly with Claude (Anthropic). It helps me to:

- turn my notes, skeletons and lab documentation into a first full draft,
- edit and polish my English (I am not a native speaker - and I prefer to keep it a bit human),
- translate fragments from Polish,
- check structure, consistency and tidy up references,
- prepare illustrations and diagrams.

Each post lists in its closing note which of these applied.

## How I review

- I read every sentence before it goes out, and I change or remove whatever is not true or not mine.
- Facts, protocol details and configuration syntax are checked against RFCs, official documentation, or my own lab.
- Where I could not verify something on a real system, I try to say so in the text.
- AI-generated illustrations are marked in their captions.

## Responsibility

I publish this blog as a private person, and I am responsible for its content - including the parts written with AI help. If you spot a mistake, it is my mistake. Let me know and I will fix it.
